Changes from the Lexicon: `demo/gol/tools/lexicon_patterns.py` extracts the
pattern diagrams and names from the plaintext release of 2019-10-29, keeps
patterns that fit a 256 x 256 grid, drops duplicates, and classifies each one
by simulating it. The Lexicon's definitions and other text are not included.
These adaptations are licensed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.

`gosper-glider-gun.cells` records the cells of the Gosper glider gun (Bill
Gosper, 1970) as shown on [LifeWiki](https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Gosper_glider_gun),
without any of LifeWiki's text; the same pattern is also part of `library.json`.

The Game of Life apps bundle `library.json` as a separate part of a larger work;
the apps' code remains under the MIT License.
