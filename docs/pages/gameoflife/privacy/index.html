本政策适用于 LazyJazzDev 开发的「生命游戏」应用（iOS 与 HarmonyOS 版本）。

我们不收集任何个人信息

本应用不需要注册或登录，不收集、不存储、不上传、也不与任何第三方共享您的个人信息。应用不包含广告、数据分析或第三方统计 SDK，也不使用网络连接。

您保存的图案

您在应用内保存的图案仅保存在本设备上属于本应用的存储空间中，不会上传。卸载应用会删除这些文件。使用「从系统文件读取」或「保存到系统文件」时，应用只访问您在系统文件选择器中主动选择的文件。

设备方向

应用读取设备方向，仅用于让界面按钮上的图标保持正向显示。该数据只在设备上即时使用，不会被记录或传输。

权限

本应用不申请任何系统权限。

儿童

本应用适合所有年龄段使用。由于我们不收集任何信息，也不会收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

English

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This policy applies to the Game of Life app (iOS and HarmonyOS) developed by LazyJazzDev.

We collect no personal information

The app requires no account or sign-in. It does not collect, store, upload, or share any personal information with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not use the network.

Your saved patterns

Patterns you save in the app stay in the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. When you choose Open from Files or Save to Files, the app accesses only the files you select in the system file picker.

Device orientation

The app reads the device orientation only to keep the icons on its buttons upright. This data is used on the device as it arrives and is never recorded or transmitted.

Permissions

The app requests no system permissions.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information, we collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For questions, contact us through GitHub Issues.
